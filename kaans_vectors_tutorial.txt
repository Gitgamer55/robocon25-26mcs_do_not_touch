correct_pos():
-Rounds the current position to basic floats or integers to make them easier to process. (simplify)

update_dir():
-Turns the current direction we are facing clockise or anticlockwise. (turn)

update_pos():
-Moves current position by the distance given in the direction we are facing. (move forward)

update_ALL():
-Updates the positions using the information given by looking at a tag. (analyse tag)

work_to_coords():
-Gives the angle required to turn and the distance required to move in order to get to the given coords.
